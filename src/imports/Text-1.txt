Banks O’ Dee Sport Club is set up a bit differently from other clubs. All company profits are re-invested back into the club to maintain, develop and improve facilities, train and develop staff to industry standard, promote and develop sports based activities and ensure we provide an excellent level of service to members. The club offers state of art facilities to cater for all fitness levels. Qualified staff provide a comprehensive induction with regular fitness testing and programming to help you achieve your personal goals.
